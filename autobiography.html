🌅 Chapter 1: The Beginning (2008)

Fig. 1.1: My childhood photo

The story begins on March 2008, when I was born.

It was a Wednesday.

On the 6th day, Mummy decided to name me with the letter 'R'.

Since Mummy's mindset wasn't clear yet, people around started suggesting names.

Like Nishant, Chinmay, blah blah...

Days passed, and everyone used to call me 'Ghantee' 😏

Actually, I am the third child after two sisters. But in Indian culture, if the 3rd child is a 'Son', he gets a different level of love.

Since childhood, I was fat, but due to my habits, I used to fall sick every month. Because of that, I started getting thin, and my face started looking real.

🕊️ Chapter 2: A Loss & A New Start (2009)

📸 Upload Chapter Photo Fig. 2.1: Add your photo description here

The first picture I have is from 2009, when my ration card was made. It was winter time, so Papa and Mummy set up a curtain in the background, and 5 family members got their photo clicked together.

I never used to make direct eye contact with anyone (as Mummy told me later).

In 2009 itself, on 12th January, my grandmother (Dadi ji) passed away due to a brain hemorrhage. I don't remember anything because I was only 11 months old.

But now I realize that I never got my grandmother's love...

Anyway, moving on from all this, life kept moving forward, and in 2009, my elder sister (the older one — Richa), got enrolled in a newly opened school called GABAS (Gautam Buddha Global School).

🏠 Chapter 3: A Changing Home (2010–2012)

📸 Upload Chapter Photo Fig. 3.1: Add your photo description here

Days kept passing, and in 2009, there were a lot of problems. First, Mummy's second issue, then Dadi's death.

Then 2010 — whether I say for myself or for my family members — must have been better.

That year, there was a wedding in the family, and my Bua ji's son was also born that year, I think.

After all this, 2011 must have been similar too. When you shift to a new house, you have to go to old festive people's place for pooja etc., so everyone used to call out my name from the road itself.

And I used to secretly watch those people.

This year, my Mundan (head-shaving ceremony) also happened on 4 May 2012, in which I obviously wasn't present (mentally/aware), because my dear ones had already left.

In 2012, my first birthday was probably celebrated on 26 March 2012, when I had perhaps turned 4 years old.

They say that when new hair grows, the mind also grows. So after my Mundan, I got attached to Anganwadi (which was Aunty's school). There, I used to count and eat soya beans.

I used to stay there till the end. And somehow, without even realizing it, I finished the Nursery syllabus.

"Swinging on the jhula has been a part of my childhood since the very beginning."

🎒 Chapter 4: First Steps into School (2013)

📸 Upload Chapter Photo Fig. 4.1: Add your photo description here

So, as I wrote, both my sisters were going to GBGS school, so the fees were 300 (for both together).

So, in February 2013, on Saraswati Puja, my admission happened directly into L.K.G (Lower Kindergarten) class.

Because I had already completed the nursery course just by swinging on the jhula.

LKG Begins...

Name — Suraj Anand
School — Gautam Buddha Global School
Sanskar Nagar, Bandwar, Begusarai
Motto — "तमसो मा ज्योतिर्गमय"

So, in the beginning of LKG, I used to cry a lot. I used to tell my sisters to come to me during lunch break.

And in class, at the end, I used to ask the other kids to let me pass.

🏚️ Chapter 5: A House Divided (2014–2016)

📸 Upload Chapter Photo Fig. 5.1: Add your photo description here

2014 — I moved to UKG.

In 2015, I was in 1st Class. Mummy used to teach at Don Bosco Academy. Due to financial constraints, I started going with her to the Don Bosco Academy (Cheria Bariyarpur, Begusarai) branch, because it was free for me there.

I went there for only 3 months, because the travel itself used to cause bone trouble, and we used to reach home only by evening.

At that time, there was a dispute in our house, and a property division happened — of land, belongings, everything.

Though our house was very big, and it was already a 2-sided house, so there was no problem.

Earlier, I used to live on the left side, then I started living on the new right side.

But even now, the financial condition kept getting worse.

In 2016, after 3 months, I continued again at GBGS.

In 2016, I did not appear for the Term-2 exam of 1st Class, because my health had deteriorated, and I was unhealthy.

'An 8-year-old boy'

Friends' names: Abhilash, Ayush, Harsh.

👦 Chapter 6: A Boy Called 'Suraj Bhaiya' (2017)

📸 Upload Chapter Photo Fig. 6.1: Add your photo description here

In 1st Class, I had many friends: Md. Nakib, Rajnish, Rupesh, Abhinav, Saurav (Komal), and many more, but...

By that time, these people left the school and went to another school, because this was a small school, and teachers were decreasing in the school.

Sandhya Miss, Puja Miss, Gunjan Sir, Jitender Sir, Chandan Sir.

The result hadn't come out yet, so my parents got me promoted from 1st Class, and since the school was my Chacha's, it happened easily.

I started a jump from 1st to 3rd initially, but in Don Bosco School, I had already studied ICSE books in 3 months, so I recovered within a few days.

In 3rd Class, 8 seniors became my friends, and from then on, they started calling me 'Suraj Bhaiya'.

So in 3rd Class, when I was going smoothly, then Nakib and Abhinav became my juniors, and then Rohit and Satyam also became juniors...

A fight broke out in our class — a random fight.

So the boys had no value in the class.

And, the teacher scolded me, and Shitanshu too, and my rank got spoiled, and Ganesh went to another school.

So from 3rd Class to 4th Class (State Board), because of Chandan Sir, everyone changed — and he made us memorize the names of state capitals.

I remember some things, so I'm writing this. Due to lack of teachers in school.

Science was taught by Veena Ma'am — about living things and all. Hindi by Sadhana Ma'am. English by Ekta Miss. Sanskrit too by Sadhana Ma'am. 3rd Class went smoothly.

📈 Chapter 7: The Desire to Rise (2018)

📸 Upload Chapter Photo Fig. 7.1: Add your photo description here

In 4th Class, I must have thought about why my ranks were coming, so I started studying — with Chanchal Sir.

Mummy got coaching arranged, but my crying superpower was very good. Whenever I didn't feel like doing any work, I used to start crying.

Teachers (2018):
Gautam Sir — English
Sunil Sir — Math
Gautam Sir / Amrender Sir — SST
Mohit Sir — English
Bittu Sir — Computer
Manish Sir — Processing

The 4th Class result came out, and at that time, my rank was 4th.

I had secured 4th, and literally, I felt bad that day.

I sat and thought that if I secured 1st in 5th Class, then in 5th my rank would be 1st.

1st: Swati
2nd: Sanjana
3rd: Asmita
4th: Suraj Anand (A.K.A Raviraj)

Then I thought — I won't follow behind others anymore. I have to push myself so far ahead, that others stay behind me.

During Holi and all, there used to be fun. To sing, we used to go to the playground and sit in a corner and listen, but friends wouldn't let us sit...

✨ Chapter 8: A Dream Takes Shape (2018–2019)

📸 Upload Chapter Photo Fig. 8.1: Add your photo description here

My Mummy joined GBGS in 2018 itself, and from then on, she used to teach Hindi in this school.

Time kept passing, and exams kept happening...

Our school had good "Sakhi" (friendship/activities), I mean, current affairs and all were very much there for everyone...

That school was only up to 8th, but the students could only reach up to 6th-7th, because after that they would leave the school — because there were no teachers.

So according to that, we (who were in 5th) became the seniors. So we 4 friends used to go to conduct the assembly.

And a girls' group + 4-5 more girls used to do the assembly.

So even in commanding, we used to have fights — "I'll do it, no I'll do it"... Bla Bla Bla.

I participated in the whole function for the first time, and that too in 5th Class...

And at that time, whatever was my dream of becoming an Engineer — that dream was going to come true anyway.

This was the first time when, on 15 August 2018, I participated in a dance and also gave a speech.

My uncle (Director Sir) gifted me a Pen, and in the assembly, he asked every child: "What will you become when you grow up?"

Someone said Doctor, someone said Police, Army, Astronaut, Scientist...

But I, Suraj Anand (Raviraj), went up and said "Engineer".

Then Sir asked: "Which one — Bridge Engineer or Computer Engineer?"

And I went silent, because I didn't even know what it takes to become an engineer!

🕯️ Chapter 9: A Tribute & A Farewell (2019)

📸 Upload Chapter Photo Fig. 9.1: Add your photo description here

I just wanted to become an engineer because my Mummy ji was an engineer.

Anyway, in March 2019, my 5th Class exam happened. But just before that, on 10 Feb 2019 (Saraswati Puja), something special happened.

In between, in Nov 2018 itself, an EC Competition (Clean & Green School) exam happened on the 14th (at Don Bosco).

It was the first time I went from home with the school kids — with friends, I had fun.

When the result came out, I realized for the first time what a competition really was. My cutoff was (85/100).

Out of them, only 51 children were selected, and the highest marks were 96.

In the evening, the management called us and gave awards to everyone. Certificate, medal, and a GK Award.

I felt happy that for the first time I got a gift and so much respect. But I felt bad that the competition was of L.K.G. level — so how would I compete?

However, on 14 Feb 2019, the Pulwama attack also happened, and the prize distribution was on the same day.

So I paid tribute to the CRPF Indian Forces, and then came back.

The final exam of Class 5th was of ICSE pattern. The result was supposed to come in the last week of March, which was on 31/03/2019.

On the first day of the class, 85%+ and second position was held by Sushil, who was my competitor.

My mood had changed, and I became diligent (hardworking) too.

My Mummy always thought well about me, and when she found out that I passed 5th Class, her happiness knew no bounds.

Since I had shifted schools, I made friends with everyone. Those who scored more than 80% were very few.

When the talk of our 8 mates came up, everyone left the school, everyone got separated, everyone started handling themselves.

What had to happen, started happening...

Because a boy who never thought... still has a long way to go.

Akhar pe hi rehta tha. Aur 'ghaahu' jhoothe kab main Nursery ka syllabus kar gaya, mujhe pata hi nahi chala.

"Jhula jhoolne ke saath bachpan se hi."
