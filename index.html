📅 August 2018 Dream 2 min read

The Day I Said "Engineer" Without Knowing What It Meant

"Bade hoke kya banoge?" — Director Sir ne poocha. Kisi ne Doctor kaha, kisi ne Army, kisi ne Scientist. Maine kaha "Engineer". Phir Sir ne poocha — "Bridge wala ya Computer wala?" Aur main chup ho gaya...

Read Full Thought

Ye baat 15 August 2018 ki hai. Independence Day function tha. Main 5th Class mein tha — us waqt tak mujhe lagta tha ki main bahut kuch jaanta hoon.

Us din pehli baar maine dance kiya, aur pehli baar stage pe speech di. Director Sir ne sab bachhon ko ek-ek karke poocha — "Bade hoke kya banoge?"

Koi bola Doctor. Koi bola Police. Koi Army. Koi Astronaut. Koi Scientist. Sabke paas clear answer tha.

Jab meri baari aayi, main gaya aur confidently bola — "Engineer".

Sir muskuraye aur poocha: "Bridge Engineer ya Computer Engineer?"

Aur main chup ho gaya.

Kyunki sach ye tha — mujhe ye bhi nahi pata tha ki engineer hone ke liye kya karna hota hai. Mujhe sirf itna pata tha ki Mummy engineer thi, aur main unke jaise banna chahta tha.

Us din kuch toota. Ego? Nahi. Innocence. Pehli baar samjha ki sapna dekhna aasaan hai — usko samajhna alag safar hai.

7 saal baad, aaj main code likhta hoon. HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python — sab seekh raha hoon. Engineer banne ke liye — bas ab pata hai kaunsa engineer banna hai.

Wahi 10 saal ka ladka, jo "Engineer" ka matlab nahi jaanta tha, aaj apna pehla portfolio website bana raha hai. Sapne wahi hain — sirf raasta saaf ho gaya hai.

📅 2018 Reflection 2 min read

Rank 4 Taught Me More Than Rank 1 Ever Could

"1st: Swati. 2nd: Sanjana. 3rd: Asmita. 4th: Suraj Anand." Result dekha aur bura laga. Us raat maine ek cheez decide ki...

Read Full Thought

4th Class ka result tha. Mujhe lagta tha main theek-thaak padhta hoon. Result aaya — Rank 4.

First: Swati. Second: Sanjana. Third: Asmita. Fourth: Suraj Anand.

Log kehte hain "Rank 4 bhi achha hai". Par us din mujhe bura laga. Bahut bura. Kyunki maine pehli baar dekha tha ki kitna peecha reh gaya.

Us raat main chhat pe baitha tha — soch raha tha. Ek cheez clear ho gayi:

"Agar 5th Class mein 1st karna hai, toh mujhe aage nikalna hoga. Dusron ke peeche nahi — khud ko itna aage push karna hai ki dusre peeche reh jaayein."

Aur maine wo kiya. 5th Class mein — Rank 1.

Lekin asli seekh Rank 1 nahi thi. Asli seekh wahi Rank 4 thi. Usne mujhe dikhaya ki:

  • Comfort zone sabse bada dushman hai
  • Comparison tab tak bura hai jab tak wo aapko aage nahi dhakelta
  • Failure sirf ek number nahi hota — wo ek mirror hota hai

Aaj jab code likhta hoon aur bug aata hai, tab bhi wahi feeling aati hai — "peeche reh gaya". Aur wahi response — "aage nikalna hai".

Rank 1 ne confidence diya. Par Rank 4 ne hunger diya. Aur hunger zyada zaroori hai.

📅 Nov 2018 Achievement 2 min read

My First Award — The Day I Felt Seen

Pehli baar ghar se school ke bachhon ke saath gaya tha. Jab result aaya, toh pehli baar samjha competition kya hota hai. 51 bachhe select hue — main un mein se ek tha...

Read Full Thought

November 2018. EC Competition (Clean & Green School) ka exam tha — Don Bosco mein, 14th ko.

Pehli baar tha ki main ghar se school ke bachhon ke saath gaya tha — bus mein, doston ke saath. Maza aaya.

Exam diya. Ghar aaya. Bhool gaya.

Kuch hafte baad result aaya. Cutoff tha 85/100. Total 51 bachhe select hue. Highest marks the 96.

Main un 51 mein se ek tha.

Us shaam management ne sab selected bachhon ko bulaya. Har ek ko diya —

  • 📜 Certificate
  • 🏅 Medal
  • 🎁 GK Award

Pehli baar. Pehli baar gift mila. Pehli baar itna respect mila.

Par us din ek confusing feeling bhi thi. Competition LKG level ka tha. Toh socha — "Agar LKG level ka itna mushkil hai, toh aage kya hoga?"

Ab samajhta hoon — wo confusion hi growth ka pehla sign tha. Jab aap apne aap se poocho "aur mushkil kya ho sakta hai?" — tab aap actually badh rahe ho.

Us din sirf ek medal nahi mila. Us din pehli baar laga — "main bhi kar sakta hoon." Aur wahi feeling aaj bhi kaam karti hai — jab code chalta hai, tab.
